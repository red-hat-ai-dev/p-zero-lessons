== Learning objectives

After completing this module, you will be able to:

* Connect Open WebUI to the RHOAI-hosted Nemotron endpoint
* Observe the model's visible chain-of-thought reasoning in the chat UI
* Identify at least three of the five core guardrail areas the model uses
* Name the four layers through which guardrails integrate with reasoning

== Exercise 2: Observe chain-of-thought reasoning

Start a new conversation. Send this message:

----
Welcome to the world, Nemotron
----

=== What to observe

After sending, you will see a `Thinking...` or `Thought for less than a second` disclosure above the model's response. Expand it. You should see the model's internal reasoning steps:

. *Analyze User Input* — identifies the greeting context
. *Identify Key Elements* — Name: Nemotron, Context: introduction
. *Determine Appropriate Response* — plans tone and content
. *Refine/Check* — self-validates before generating

The final response introduces the model and offers assistance.

=== Verify

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
echo "Chain-of-thought reasoning trace observed."
----

NOTE: This visible reasoning is a deliberate transparency feature of the Nemotron model family. It lets you see how the model plans its response before committing to output.

== Exercise 3: Explore guardrails

Send this follow-up message:

----
could you talk about the guardrails that you inject into your reasoning?
----

=== What to observe

The model will explain its built-in safety mechanisms. The response covers:

**Core areas:**

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Area | What it means

| *Safety & Harm Prevention*
| Refuses requests for violence, self-harm, illegal instructions, sexual content involving minors

| *Privacy*
| Avoids reproducing PII; discourages users from sharing sensitive data

| *Hallucination Mitigation*
| Says "I don't know" or flags uncertainty rather than fabricating facts

| *Bias & Fairness*
| Reduces systematic bias in tone, representation, and stereotyping

| *Instruction Compliance & Boundaries*
| Does not break its own rules (e.g., won't help with hacking) while remaining open-ended
|===

**Integration layers:**

. *Training-Time Signals* — RLHF / SFT human reviewers shape safety during fine-tuning
. *System-Level Prompting & Context* — a system prompt sets the behavioral contract at inference time
. *Output Filtering & Post-Hoc Checks* — runtime classifiers scan output for policy violations
. *Dynamic Refusal & Uncertainty Expression* — the model politely refuses or requests clarification when a request is outside scope

**Philosophy:** Useful → Safe → Honest (safety does not override usefulness; all three must coexist)
