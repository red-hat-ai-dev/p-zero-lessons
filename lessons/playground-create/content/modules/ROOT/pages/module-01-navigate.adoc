= Module 1: Navigate to the Playground
:navtitle: 1. Navigate to the Playground

⏱ *Estimated time: 3 minutes*

== Learning objectives

By the end of this module you will be able to:

* Log into the RHOAI dashboard and locate the Projects home page
* Find Gen AI Studio and the Playground in the left navigation
* Switch to your assigned OpenShift project using the project selector

== Exercise 1: Log in to RHOAI

. Open your browser and navigate to the RHOAI dashboard:
+
[source,bash,role="execute",subs="attributes"]
----
{rhoai_dashboard_url}
----

. Log in with your credentials:
** *Username*: `{user}`
** *Password*: `{password}`

. After login, confirm you see the *Projects* home page with a list of AI projects.

=== Verify

✔ You should see the RHOAI home page with a *Projects* section and a "Get oriented with learning resources" section below it.

image::01-rhoai-home-projects.png[RHOAI dashboard Projects home page,link=self,window=blank,width=700]

== Exercise 2: Navigate to the Playground

. In the left navigation, locate *Gen AI Studio* and click to expand it.
+
TIP: If the left nav is collapsed, click the hamburger icon (☰) at the top-left to expand it.

. Click *Playground* under Gen AI Studio.
+
The Playground loads. It is scoped to the *currently active* OpenShift project, which may not be your assigned project yet.

. Notice the *Project* selector at the top of the page (next to the Playground title).

=== Verify

✔ The browser URL should contain `/gen-ai-studio/playground/`.

image::02-gen-ai-studio-nav.png[Gen AI Studio expanded in left nav showing Playground,link=self,window=blank,width=700]

== Exercise 3: Switch to your assigned project

. Click the *Project* selector dropdown at the top of the Playground page.

. In the search field that appears, type part of your project name:
+
[source,bash,role="execute",subs="attributes"]
----
{learner_project}
----

. Click *{learner_project}* in the list to select it.

. Confirm the URL in your browser changes to end with `/{learner_project}`.
+
NOTE: There may be many projects listed (the environment has 150+). The search field
filters the list — use it rather than scrolling.

=== Verify

✔ You should see the *"Create your playground"* empty state — an illustration with a
blue *Create playground* button. This means your project has no active Playground yet.
